### "Where do I put the URL?"
→ In `index.html`, find two places with:
```html
action="https://script.google.com/macros/d/YOUR_GOOGLE_APPS_SCRIPT_URL_HERE/usercontent"
```
Replace `YOUR_GOOGLE_APPS_SCRIPT_URL_HERE` with your actual URL from Google Apps Script.
